1005 Intelligence Labs · hiring puzzle 016 · AI/ML + Data Engineer

The logger says fine. The insulin disagrees.

Four reefer trucks carried cold-chain pharma for our ERP customer yesterday. The temperature loggers in all four say nothing went above 8 °C. One customer is certain their cargo spoiled. They are right.

Find the shipment that breached, when the cargo air first crossed 8.0 °C, and how hot it got.

Rules of the domain

  • Breach = cargo air at or above 8.0 °C for 10 or more continuous minutes. Shorter excursions are logged as near-misses, not breaches.
  • Exactly one of the four shipments breached.
  • The loggers are not broken. They are doing exactly what the datasheet says.

The files

FileWhat it is
shipments.csvthe four shipments: route, cargo, logger, limit
telemetry.csv2,400 rows, one per minute per shipment: logger reading and ambient temperature along the route
stops.csvevery stop where the doors opened, with door-open and door-close times
loggers.csvfleet logger register: model, firmware, unit, mounting, calibration certificate
CAL-TL25-B07.csvbatch calibration certificate for the loggers
DATASHEET.mdthe logger datasheet

Ambient temperatures are from the Open-Meteo ERA5 archive (CC BY 4.0) for the route cities, 14 Oct 2025. Everything else is synthetic.

BASE=https://1005systems.github.io/hiring-portal/cold-chain
for f in shipments.csv telemetry.csv stops.csv loggers.csv CAL-TL25-B07.csv DATASHEET.md; do curl -sO $BASE/$f; done

How to play

  • Any language, any tool, any agent. We expect you to use one; we want to see how you drove it.
  • Five minutes is the budget. Over is fine, tell us how long.
  • Nothing you do can break it. Files are static and read-only.
